## Needs design

1. **Fleet Space Stations.** Free built-in Features, a number of extra Features
   the station must take, optional Armaments (weapons repeatable, Structures one
   each, Defensive Garrison and Forward Operating Base exclusive), and upgrades
   (Astrobotanical Lab, Defence Grid, or one Feature). The picker today has one
   armament pool and one upgrade pool.
2. **Escort (14.1.6).** Chosen before deployment: an Escort Group is paired with
   one H or C Group and they activate together. Nothing in the list sets a pair.
3. **Shield-X (14.1.16).** A shields token raised at the start of an activation:
   saves replaced, Signature tripled, a Spike after each attack, lost on Silent
   Running. Calculator and play mode have no toggle.
