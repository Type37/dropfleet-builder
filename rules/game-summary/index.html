5 GAME ROUND SEQUENCE
6 Planning Phase
1) Ability Point Generation
2) Generate Pass Token
3) Determine Initiative
7 Activation Phase

1) Activate Groups

Players alternate activating Groups in initiative order or using a pass token

Sequence: Group Order>Move>Attack>Launch

8 Asset Phase

1) Battalion Combat

In Initiative order, players select a Dropsite with opposing Battalions and resolve its combat:

  • Dropsite to Feature
  • Dropsite Centric
  • Feature Centric
  • Feature Demolition
  • Battalion Consolidation

Remove Battalions equally until a single players Battalions remain or none remain. Battalions equal to the Hull of a Feature with no enemy Battalions may be removed to destroy it.

Activate Dropsite

The controlling player attacks and/or launches with Scenario Features only they have Battalions on, then each player activates their deployed Features.

2) Boarding Actions

Battalions on a Ship are removed equally first. Battalion Carriers that did not Launch may use a chosen Asset that deploys Battalions to remove that many enemy Battalions from the ship. Enemy Battalions remaining on Ships inflict Core hits equal to their amount; each passed Backup save also removes a Battalion. Ships destroyed this way do not roll for explosion and are worth double their points.

3) Asset Combat

Sequence: Fighters>Bombers>Torpedoes

Player activates all Assets of the same type: all form Wings, all move, then all simultaneously attack. Then proceed to the next player

9 End Phase

1) Repair

Roll 1D6 for each repairable Crippling Effect, on a 4+ remove that effect (Fire 3+, Orbital Decay 5+)

2) Victory Points3) Cleanup

7.3 Attack Summary

1) Determine Potential Targets

  • Verify Formation Benefits

2) Assign Weapons to Targets

3) Roll to Hit

  • Target is Descent Ship in Atmosphere, Lock 6+
  • Target is Non-Descent Ship in Atmosphere, reduce Lock by 1
  • Escape Velocity treats Descent Ships as Non-Descent Ships
  • Bombardment…
    • Ignore penalties when Target in Atmosphere
    • Target is City, improve Lock by 2
    • Target in Orbit from Orbit, Lock 6+
    • cannot assign to Target in Orbit from Atmosphere
  • Verify other Special Rules (Air to Air, Close Action…)

4) Roll to Save (against Hits)

  • Check Fighter–Close Protection
  • Check other Special Rules (Aegis, Shield, Scald…)

5) Inflict Damage

  • Highest Damage hits to lowest Hull Ship first

6) Roll Crippling Effects (Capital Ships Only)

  • In Formation, 10+ is Structural Damage

7) Roll Explosions (Capital Ships Only)

  • Range 3/6/9
  • Heavy Ships add 1 to result
  • Only affects Ships in same layer
  • Colossal Ships add 2 to the result

14.1 Ship Special Rules Summary

14.1.1 Aegis-X

All Ships with this rule in a Group in coherency combine their X values into a single value Y.

Friendly Groups in coherency within 6” of a Ship with this rule and the same Orbital Layer (including its own Group) gain the benefits of Aegis.

When rolling saves against Close Action and Bomber (of any type) attacks, those Groups gain Y additional save dice they may add to their Energy or Kinetic saves. Before rolling to save, the defending player assigns these dice against specific weapons, divided up as they choose (including any modified hits) between any Energy or Kinetic saves.

An Aegis Group can only grant the benefits of Aegis once each time a friendly group is attacked.

A friendly Group can only benefit from Aegis from one Group each time it is attacked.

Aegis has no effect on Backup saves or saves that replace Energy and Kinetic saves (such as Shield saves).

In addition, Ships benefiting from Aegis increase the number of bombers required to trigger Saturation Fire by Y.

14.1.5 Detector

If this Ship is on an order that allows it to attack, this Ship may forgo one allowed Weapon use during its activation. If it does, at the end of its activation place two Spikes on an enemy Group in line of sight. A Group that uses Detector gains a Spike (regardless of the amount of Ships using this rule).

14.1.6 Escort

At the start of the game, before deployment, you may assign this Group to escort a friendly Group of H or C Tonnage. An Escort Group and its escorted Group are a pair, no other Groups can be part of this pair. Until either Group is destroyed, the pair activate and perform their actions (moving, turning, attacking, etc) simultaneously and count as a single Group when generating pass tokens. The pair do not need to choose the same Order when they activate. Escort Groups are deployed alongside their escorted Group, ignoring tonnage restrictions for the Escort Group. The pair do not need to be in coherency, however while both Groups are within 6” of each other they ignore Saturation Fire from enemy Bombers.

14.1.7 Gateship-X

Shaltari Motherships must launch their Dropships via a Ship/Dropsite with this rule (sometimes referred to as Gateships). Each Gateship can have up to X Dropships deployed from it each round. Note: though the launch is measured from the Gateship, it is the Mothership that is launching.

When this Ship goes on the Max Thrust or Damage Control orders, it reduces its Gateship-X value to 0 until the start of its next activation.

14.1.10 Mothership

Shaltari Motherships can only deploy their Dropships via friendly Ships or controlled Dropsites with the Gateship-X special rule (sometimes referred to as Gateships).

These Gateships must be within 18” of the Mothership or part of a chain of Gateships with each Gateship in the chain being within 18” of another. Note: though the launch is measured from the Gateship, it is the Mothership that is launching.

14.1.11 Payload S/L-X

During the Porter Ship’s activation, if it is on an Order that allows it to Launch, you may detach one or more of its Payload Ships and place them within 3” of it. In the round they are detached, these Payloads activate together immediately as part of the Porter Ship’s activation after it has completed its Order. In subsequent rounds, all your Payload Ships count as being part of a single Group. Crippling Effects, Spikes, and other rules that affect an entire Group only affect the targeted Payload Ship.

Payload Ships always follow General Quarters Orders, even if its Porter is on different Orders. Payload Ships ignore Coherency rules (and do not gain Formation Benefits), have no Lead Ship, are targeted by attacks individually, and these attacks can only damage the targeted Payload Ship.

If a Payload Ship is attached to a Porter Ship when that Ship is destroyed, the Payload Ship is destroyed.

14.1.12 Porter S/L-X

This Ship may be assigned Payload Ships with the same letter up to a total capacity of X. During a Porter’s activation, it may attach any detached Payload Ships within 3” if it has available capacity. Payload Ships reattached to a Porter Ship are removed from the table to be deployed in a later round.

Any tokens, Spikes, or effects affecting the reattached Payload Ship are removed from that Ship.

14.1.16 Shield-X

This Group/Dropsite has an unmodifiable Shield save of X+.

At the start of this Group’s or controlled Dropsite’s activation, you may choose to raise its shields. If you do, this group gains a shields token until the start of its next activation.

While a Group/Dropsite has a shields token, it replaces its Energy and Kinetic saves with its Shield save and triples its Signature value (before Spikes). Shield saves are used against all hits from weapons before making Backup saves. Shield saves must be used before making Backup saves against Core hits. After being attacked, a Group with a shields token gains a Spike if at least one attack dice hits.

A Group on Silent Running loses any shields tokens it has and cannot have shields tokens.

Groups that Directly Deploy may start the game with a shields token.

Core Hits caused by Boarding Actions and Saturation Fire cannot be saved by Shield Saves.

14.1.20 Impetuous

At the start of each round, friendly Groups with this rule improve the Lock of their Weapon Systems by 1 until you activate a friendly Group without this rule.

14.2 Weapon Special Rules Summary

14.2.6 Bombardment

This Weapon ignores the penalties for attacking Cities and Ships in Atmosphere. Improve the Lock of this Weapon by 2 when attacking Cities. When attacking a Ship in Orbit with this weapon, it always hits on a 6+ and can never cause a critical. This weapon cannot be used to attack Ships in Orbit while the attacking Ship is in Atmosphere.

14.2.7 Burnthrough-X

Each of this Weapon’s critical hits reduces the target’s Energy/Kinetic Save by X for hits caused by this Weapon for this attack. Saves made for other Weapons are unaffected. Roll attacks separately for each Burnthrough Weapon.

14.2.8 Calibre-X

Improve the Lock value of this Weapon by 1 when attacking Groups of any of the listed X Tonnage values.

14.2.9 Close Action

This weapon does not benefit from Formation Benefits and can only be assigned to targets within Scan range and on the same Orbital Layer as the attacker.

14.2.10 Corruptor-X

When this Weapon successfully inflicts damage, place X Battalions on the target.

14.2.12 Critical-X

Each of this Weapon’s criticals increases the damage of that hit by X.

14.2.13 Escape Velocity

This weapon ignores the penalty for attacking Ships in Orbit and treats all Ships in Atmosphere as non-Descent Ships, so only reduces its Lock by 1 when attacking Ships in Atmosphere. Close Action weapons with this special rule may be used against targets in Orbit while the attacking Ship is in Atmosphere.

14.2.14 Flash-X

When this Weapon inflicts damage, the damaged Group (or Ship if it ignores the usual coherency rules) gains X Spikes.

14.2.15 Focused

Instead of the normal potential targets for Weapons, this Weapon’s potential targets are either:

  • A single enemy Ship.
  • A Dropsite containing no friendly Battalions.

Damage caused by this Weapon is allocated to only the target.

14.2.16 Fusillade-X

This Weapon increases its Attack value by X while its Ship is on Weapons Free.

14.2.20 Linked-X

Weapons with the same Linked-X value on the same Ship count as 1 Weapon for Orders purposes. When you attack with a Linked-X Weapon, you attack with all other Weapons on that Ship with the same Linked-X value.

14.2.23 Overcharge

Before assigning Weapons to targets, you may double the Damage value of this Weapon. If you do, this becomes a High-Power Weapon for this activation and each attack roll of 6 causes that attacking Ship to lose this Weapon’s unmodified Damage value in hull points. Only one Weapon per Ship may benefit from this rule per activation.

14.2.26 Scald-X

If this weapon attacks a target within Scan range of the attacking Ship, reduce the target’s Energy / Kinetic save against this weapon’s hits by X (other attacks and weapons are unaffected).

14.2.27 Status

When you hit with this Weapon, pick one of the following tokens; Defence Systems Offline, Scanners Offline, or Navigation Offline.

At the end of the Inflict Damage step, for each unsaved hit from this weapon, choose one of the defending Ships to gain the selected token, regardless of the amount of damage caused by those hits.

This token is automatically removed at the end of the affected ship’s next activation or the start of the next End Phase, before making repairs (whichever is first).

Tokens gained from this rule are counted separately from normal Crippling Effects. A Ship may only have one of each type of these tokens gained due to this rule.

14.2.29 Volley-X

This Weapon counts as 1 Weapon System for Orders purposes. When you assign this Weapon to a target, you assign it to targets up to X times (either the same or different). Once assigned, treat each allocation as a separate weapon for the subsequent steps in the attack sequence.

Broadside Weapons using Volley must alternate arcs when determining and assigning targets (so cannot target the same Group on the same side twice in a row).

14.2.30 Reave-X

Each of this Weapon’s criticals reduces the Energy/Kinetic save used against that hit by X.

14.2.31 Anti Wing

This weapon may target enemy Wings as if they were Ships (with a Sig of 0”) but reduces its Lock value by 1 for that attack. If it does, for each hit, remove 1 Fighter or 1 Bomber from that Wing or any other enemy Wing within 3” of the target.

When this weapon is assigned to a Torpedo, it reduces its Lock value by 1 instead of always hitting on a 6+.
